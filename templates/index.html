          About the campus data

          Layout: every room box is copied from the floor-plan PDF (Gedung A floors 1-6, Gedung B floors 2-7 and 9 including the rear "Belakang" wings, Gedung C floors 8, 11 and 12). Positions, widths, depths and labels are the PDF's.

          Estimated size: the PDF has no scale, so box sizes are converted to metres with one factor per plan type. A typical classroom comes out at about 7 x 9 m. Change S_MAIN and S_WING in campus_data.py to re-scale.

          Generated: corridors (the free space between rooms, turned into a 2 m walking grid), the door of each room, lift / stairs connections between floors, the ground floors of Gedung B and C (no plan in the PDF), the lobbies and the outdoor walkway from the Main Gate.
